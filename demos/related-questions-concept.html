What is the Surfing Pikachu card?

Surfing Pikachu is a special variant Pokemon card first introduced in the original base set era. It features Pikachu riding a surfboard and has the unique "Surf" attack that deals 30 damage.

The card has been reprinted in several sets over the years and is notable for being one of the earliest "alternate art" style cards in the TCG.

What sets has it appeared in?

Surfing Pikachu has appeared in several Pokemon TCG sets across multiple generations:

Original releases: Wizards Black Star Promos (#28), Pokemon Stadium bundle promo, and the Japanese Vending Machine series.

Modern reprints: XY — Evolutions (2016) brought it back as a nostalgia reprint, and most recently it appeared in the Celebrations 25th Anniversary set (2021) as a Classic Collection reprint.

The card has maintained its iconic "Surf" attack across all printings, though the artwork and HP values have varied between versions.
